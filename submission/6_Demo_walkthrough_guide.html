End-to-End Orchestration Challenge · Demo Runbook

SESAP — one guided run that proves the whole loop

A support lead acts in the app, Maestro orchestrates, the robot does the work and writes back to Data Fabric, and the app reflects it. Follow the steps in order — each is tagged with the exact requirement or bonus it demonstrates on camera.

Coded App→ Maestro (BPMN)→ RPA robot→ Data Fabric ↺  app reflects the change

Before you hit record

Five minutes of setup makes the take clean. Do these first.

  • Turn Live automation ON — Settings → Automation & Robot Units. This lets a click actually fire Maestro + the robot + emails.
  • Open the app fresh — new/incognito window, sign in again so you're on the latest revision. Sign in as the Supervisor (admin / sesap2026).
  • Resolve one or two tickets first (off-camera) — this seeds the Avg Resolution KPI with a real value before you show Analytics.
  • Have two files ready to drag in: a clear photo of an ID card and a PDF (statement or receipt).
  • Do one silent dry-run: resolve a ticket, confirm the email lands (check spam); read one ID image in the copilot. If either misbehaves, fix it — don't record around it.
  • Second tab on Orchestrator (Maestro monitoring + Jobs) so you can cut to the running instance without hunting.
